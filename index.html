Adaptive procedure

The clinician sets the starting level; the adaptive procedure moves the presentation level after each word to home in on the 50% speech reception threshold (SRT), then fits a psychometric function for the SRT and slope.

A1 default 20 trials (2 lists of 10); A2 default 30 trials (3 lists of 10).

Brand & Kollmeier parameters

These constants are shared by A1 and A2 and by quiet and noise conditions; the defaults follow Brand & Kollmeier (2002). You rarely need to change them.
